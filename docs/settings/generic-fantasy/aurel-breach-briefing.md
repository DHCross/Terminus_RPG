# Guide's Briefing Sheet: The Aurel Crossing

> **A playable transition scenario** mapping a silent Tringad summons hall into
> the sunlit, unanchored realm of Aurel. Demonstrates how the pacing shifts when a municipal
> response cell investigates a boundary fracture and steps through into the older world.
> **Audience:** Guide only.

---

## Briefing Overview

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **Setting** | Tringad (Sumner's Ward) → Aurel (The Sunlit Reach) |
| **Party** | 3–5 licensed responders (Order cell) |
| **Playtime** | 3–4 hours (one session) |
| **Structure** | Investigation → Crossing → Rupture Containment |
| **Core Tension** | In Aurel, Drift is the weather (+1 every round). Contain the anomaly and extract before the clock runs out. |
| **Tone Shift** | Cold brass & rain-slicked ledgers → sunlit tall grass & careless mythic magic |

---

## Act 1: The Tringad Side — "The Quiet Summons Office"

### Origination Locale
**Sumner's Ward, Old Consignment Row.** A drafty, rain-soaked municipal summons office that has stood vacant since the winter freeze. The rain cuts against leaded glass windows. An iron ledger desk sits in the corner with oxblood ink dried into the wood. The Arch-Sumner's bells sound the fourth hour, but from the back archive chamber, no bell echoes back.

### The Call
The cell is dispatched because the consignment office failed to acknowledge its morning routine check. The ledger records have drifted: names stamped on the intake slips do not match the tollgate tallies at the crossing. A Seeker has flagged it as an **accumulated boundary fracture**.

### Scene 1: The Barred Archive Door (Tringad Rules)

**Ground:** The archive door is reinforced with cold iron bands and held by an ancient municipal seal. The floorboards are damp from rain leaking through the eaves. Words spoken in the room carry a dull echo.

**Will:** The municipal seal is holding the boundary shut. Forcing the door without addressing the seal requires winning a Force exchange against the door's Endure (d8).

**Shift:**
- If the cell inspects the intake slips, a Seeker identifies the last recorded clerk: *Master Vareth*.
- If a Breaker applies Force to the hinges, the iron screams and cracks open, but increases scene tension.
- If a Broker or Shade examines the lock seam, they find the keyhole is packed with dried summer grass and flower pollen instead of dust.

**Drift (Hesitation Engine):** 
- At Drift 1: The rain against the window stops making sound.
- At Drift 2: Warm air begins blowing from under the archive door, smelling of clover and sun-baked stone.
- At Drift 3: The shadows in the corner stretch toward the door instead of away from the lantern.

---

## Scene 2: The Fracture (The Crossing)

Beyond the door, the stone masonry does not lead to a basement. The back wall of the archive is split open—not by an explosion, but by a clean vertical gap where the stones have forgotten how to touch.

Through the gap, golden sunlight pours across the flagstones. The scent of rain and cold ink vanishes, replaced by warm wind, wild honey, and tall green grass.

**Ground:** The gap is wide enough for one person to step through at a time. The stone edges are warm to the touch.

**Will:** The warm air is blowing outward into the rain. The sunlit valley on the other side is in active motion.

**Shift:** When the first cell member steps through the gap, they cross the threshold diegetically into **Aurel**.

---

## Act 2: The Aurel Side — "The Sunlit Valley"

### Read-Aloud on Crossing
> *"You step through the broken masonry and the world turns warm. The cold rain and dark slate of Tringad are gone. You stand on the crest of a green hillside under a brilliant, cloudless sky. Tall grass ripples in the wind down to a sun-drenched valley.*
>
> *In the distance stands an open stone tower of white granite, surrounded by silver-barked trees. Above the tower, brilliant arcs of crimson and sapphire light flare across the sky—careless, massive magical displays that no sanctioned caster in Tringad would dare unleash.*
>
> *There are no bells here. No tollgates. No ledgers. And at the bottom of your sight, the scene's Drift counter is already ticking."*

---

### How the Rules Change in Aurel

| Mechanic | Tringad (The City) | Aurel (The Sunlit Reach) |
|---|---|---|
| **Drift Pacing** | **Hesitation Drift:** advances only when the cell delays or fails | **Ambient Drift:** advances automatically at the end of every round (the weather) |
| **Magic Economy** | Sanctioned Workings cost 1+ Exert to keep Drift quiet | Local casters cast **Unanchored Workings** (0 Exert, but pushes Drift +1 or +2) |
| **The Stakes** | Restore routine; maintain the quiet day | Stop the unanchored caster, bridge the gap, and extract before Drift reaches 6 |
| **The Countdown** | No fixed round limit | **At Drift 6:** The local valley fractures completely into uncontrolled rupture |

---

### Scene 3: The White Granite Tower

The stone tower belongs to **Vareth**, an exiled scholar-mage of Aurel's *Esoteric Arts*. Vareth has been hurling unanchored *Nullify* and *Bridge* Workings into the upper sky, attempting to breach the boundary into what he believes is a mythic realm of order. His reckless casting is the reason the summons office in Sumner's Ward cracked open.

**Ground:** The open courtyard is covered in white granite paving stones split by creeping vines. A flight of stone steps ascends to the open roof where Vareth is casting.

**Will:** Vareth is obsessed with his experiment. Every round, he channels an unanchored Working at the sky, adding **+1 or +2 to the scene's Drift**.

**Shift:**
- If a cell member confronts Vareth with words, his attention wavers for one round, delaying his next casting.
- If a Warden steps between Vareth and the sky, an *Anchor Point* ability can intercept his casting beam.
- If a Breaker targets Vareth's focus staff with *Break the Tool*, destroying the staff ends his ability to cast unanchored spells.

**Drift Ladder (The Valley Clock):**
- **Drift 1–2 (Baseline):** The air is warm; grass grows visibly underfoot.
- **Drift 3:** Arcs of sapphire static dance across metal armor. Gravity lightens slightly.
- **Drift 4:** Distance stretches; shouting across the courtyard arrives before lips finish moving.
- **Drift 5:** The stone paving fractures into floating shards. Wild elemental sparks manifest.
- **Drift 6 (Collapse):** The valley destabilizes into an active Rupture cascade. The cell has 2 rounds to reach the crossing gap or be trapped in the collapsing realm.

---

### Key Aurel Figures

| NPC | Legacy | Role | Motivation |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Vareth** | The Esoteric Arts | The Caster (Antagonist) | Believes breaking the sky will reveal a perfect city of silver and law. Desperate and reckless. |
| **Sister Lyra** | The Sacred Covenants | The Healer (Ally) | Bound by **The Edgeless Vow**. She tends to Vareth's exhausted acolytes in the lower crypts. Knows how to perform a two-sided *Seal* Working to close the fracture. |
| **The Gate Warden** | The Vanguard | Courtyard Anchor | An armored warrior holding the courtyard steps. Braced against intrusion; can be negotiated with via a Broker or bypassed via a Shade's *Unmarked Entry*. |

---

## Act 3: The Seal & Extraction

### Scene 4: Sealing the Fracture
To close the boundary gap before the valley reaches Drift 6:
1. **Subdue or Convince Vareth:** Stop the unanchored casting through dialogue, a Willpower contest, or disarming his focus staff.
2. **The Two-Sided Seal:**
   - **Tringad Side:** One responder steps back into the consignment office and anchors a **Seal Working** on the municipal ledger desk (costs 1 Exert).
   - **Aurel Side:** Sister Lyra (or a cell caster) anchors a matching **Seal Working** on the white granite masonry (costs 1 Exert).
3. **Extraction:** Once both seals are declared, the cell has 1 round to step through the gap before it snaps shut, knitting the stone walls together.

### Closing Read-Aloud
> *"You step back across the threshold into the dark consignment room. The heat and golden sun vanish in a breath. Cold rain beats against the leaded glass. The iron smell of wet ink returns.*
>
> *Behind you, the gap in the stone masonry seals with a dull, heavy thud. The flagstones settle. The Arch-Sumner's bells ring the fifth hour, clear and rhythmic across the rooftops.*
>
> *The ledger on the desk is dry. The Quiet Day has returned to Sumner's Ward."*

## Guide's Quick-Reference Summary

```plain
AUREL CROSSING MECHANICS

Tringad Engine:
  - Skills vs. Thresholds (Core Exchange)
  - Hesitation Drift (ticks only on delay/failure)
  - Sanctioned Workings (cost Exert; Drift stays 0)

Aurel Engine:
  - Exact same Skill/Threshold dice
  - Ambient Drift: +1 every round automatically
  - Unanchored Workings: 0 Exert, +1 or +2 Drift per cast
  - Drift 6: Region destabilizes; 2 rounds to extract

Four Verbs in Play:
  - Seal: Lock the boundary gap shut (1 Exert)
  - Expose: Reveal Vareth's focus staff / hidden crypts
  - Bridge: Step across fractured stone shards
  - Nullify: Dispel Vareth's static field or break his staff
```
