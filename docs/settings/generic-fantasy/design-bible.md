# Aurel (Generic Fantasy) — Setting Design Bible (Guide Only)

> **Status:** Living document. Captures the narrative spine, world framing,
> and mechanical integration of the Aurel setting for the Coherence System.
> **Audience:** Guide only. Characters can visit Aurel; they cannot learn what it is.

---

## 1. What Aurel Is: The Unanchored World

Beneath Tringad's severity lies an older, warmer version of the world.

**Aurel** is sunlit and careless: mythic kingdoms, open horizons, tall grass, and magic used freely by people who never anchored any of it. It was not destroyed. It was set aside and left running, sealed off, because it never finished failing.

Aurel is where swords and sorcery lives. It is a real place with real people who believe their world is the only one, and it is dying slowly because nothing there repeats.

### 1.1 The Pacing Engine: Drift as the Weather

- Aurel has no bells, no tollgates, no ledgers, no synchronized sequence. Nothing holds it in place.
- **In Tringad, Drift advances when the cell hesitates.**
- **In Aurel, Drift climbs on its own, every round, whether or not anyone acts.**
- Drift is not a sudden accident—it is the ambient climate of the realm. A scene set in Aurel runs against a visible, rising Drift counter on the table.

### 1.2 The Diegetic Crossing

A cell investigating a fractured boundary in Sumner's Ward traces it to a silent, drafty consignment hall or a summons office nobody has been assigned to in years. There is a gap in the stone that is not a door.

They step through it and leave the rain, oxblood ink, and bone paper behind for sun on tall grass.

## 2. The Four Legacies

In Aurel, characters do not join licensed civic response Orders. They inherit ancient, mythic **Legacies**—living traditions of martial and magical practice.

| Legacy | Practice & Field Function |
|---|---|
| **The Vanguard** | Holds ground by presence and arms. Steps into harm and stays standing. |
| **The Esoteric Arts** | Works magic openly and unanchored, at no visible personal cost. |
| **The Infiltration Arts** | Lives in the gaps of attention. Traps, hidden entries, and reversals. |
| **The Sacred Covenants** | Seals boundaries and mends what is broken, under strict sworn vow. |

### 2.1 The Edgeless Vow (The Sacred Covenants)

A Covenant holds their power by refusing the blade. Edged steel is intent to spill blood, and a hand that has taken up an edge cannot also close a wound. 

Covenants carry staves, maces, hammers, and chains: tools that break, ward, and bind, but do not cut. A Covenant who draws a sword does not lose a rule—they lose the ability to perform mending Workings until the vow is made good again in the fiction.

## 3. Origins as Conditional Trait Exceptions

Origins (lineages) in Aurel do not grant passive stat bloat. They provide distinct conditional permissions that interact directly with the scene:

| Origin | Trait | Mechanical Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **Human** | Flexible Training | Once per scene, step up one Skill roll by one die rank if the action aligns with the character's Legacy or Order. |
| **Dwarf** | Subterranean Anchor | +1 Threshold Circle in Endure. Ignore Ground restrictions from heavy armor, encumbrance, or unstable footing. |
| **Elf** | Deep Attunement | +1 Threshold Circle in Avoid. Cast Sanctioned Workings using natural elements (wood, wind, water) without requiring manufactured focus tools. |
| **Halfling** | Slip Focus | Once per scene, when a hostile consequence or attack targets the party, slip from view and redirect the immediate threat to an obstacle or braced ally. |
| **Stoneborn** | Hard Memory | Once per scene, ignore the first lost Endure circle from environmental collapse, crushing pressure, or forced movement. |

## 4. Magic in Aurel: The Four Verbs Unanchored

Aurel uses the exact same closed Four-Verb magic engine as Tringad (§12). There is no separate spell list, no mana, and no spell slots.

### 4.1 The Four Controlled Verbs

| Verb | Effect in Aurel | Example Castings |
|---|---|---|
| **Seal** | Lock a state in place: hold a gate shut, mend a wound, sustain a protective aura. | *Gate-Lock*, *Coherence Field*, *Structure Lock* |
| **Expose** | Make hidden truth or weak points legible: reveal invisible wards, illusions, or tracks. | *Arcane Sight*, *Ward Sight*, *Trace Path* |
| **Bridge** | Connect two related things across distance or substance. | *Gate-Step* (spatial jump), *Bless* (bridge divine protection) |
| **Nullify** | Remove an existing permission or barrier: shatter wards, dispel bindings, corrode armor. | *Breach Writ*, *Rust-Wake* (corrode barrier/gear) |

### 4.2 The Unanchored Trap

The difference between Tringad and Aurel is not the verbs—it is **who pays**:

- **Tringad Casters (Sanctioned):** Pay **1 or more Exert circles** to anchor the Working. Scene Drift stays quiet (+0).
- **Aurel Casters (Unanchored):** Pay **0 Exert**. They draw power directly from the surrounding world, causing the scene's **Drift to spike (+1 or +2)** with every spell.

To a visiting cell, local wizards look terrifyingly powerful for ten minutes—freely hurling lightning and transmuting stone without breaking a sweat. But every unanchored casting accelerates the realm toward systemic collapse. The visitors' disciplined Exert economy is the only reason they survive the extraction.

## 5. Core Rules & Conflict Mechanics

Aurel runs on the exact same paired-dice engine as Tringad without conversion.

### 5.1 Paired Skills and Thresholds

| Action Skill | Resisting Threshold | Direction |
|---|---|---|
| **Force** | **Endure** | Act with force / absorb impact on body, mass, or armor |
| **Agility** | **Avoid** | Act with precision / evade pressure via movement and positioning |
| **Willpower** | **Exert** | Act through resolve / spend inner reserve to resist strain or fuel magic |

**Threshold Circles** are derived strictly from the linked Skill die:
- `d4 = 1 circle`
- `d6 = 2 circles`
- `d8 = 3 circles`
- `d10 = 4 circles`
- `d12 = 5 circles`

### 5.2 The Core Exchange
1. Acting side rolls **Skill** (Force, Agility, or Willpower).
2. Responding side rolls **Threshold** (Endure, Avoid, or Exert).
3. Higher roll takes control of the exchange.
4. Consequence resolves: the loser marks off Threshold circles equal to the weapon's **Impact** (1–3), modified by special **Vectors**.

### 5.3 Armament: Impact and Vectors

Weapons do not roll separate damage dice. They deliver **Impact** and apply **Vectors**:

| Weapon / Tool | Impact | Vector |
|---|---|---|
| Dagger / Knife | 1 | armor-piercing |
| Shortsword / Dueling Blade | 2 | quick |
| Longsword / Battleaxe | 2 | balanced |
| Spear / Halberd | 2 | reaches position |
| Heavy Crossbow | 2 | armor-piercing |
| Greatsword / Greataxe | 3 | hard to Avoid |
| War Hammer / Heavy Mace | 3 | breaks protection |
| Staff | 1 | reach / warding |
| Shield | 1 | protects position |

## 6. Signature Keys: Iconic Gear

A **Signature Key** is the defining physical tool that makes a hero legible in the scene.

### 6.1 Categories
- **Signature Implement:** A weapon or tool exerting force (e.g., *Oathkeeper* greatsword). Grants +1 Impact or stepped-up die on aligned exchanges.
- **Working Anchor:** A focus holding Workings in place (e.g., *The Last Lamp of Aurel*).
- **Resonance Key:** An item of continuity stabilizing resolve (e.g., *The Iron Vow Chain*). Once per scene, allows restoring an Avoid circle or stepping up Willpower.
- **Unified Signature:** Combines an Implement and an Anchor (e.g., *Moon-Glass Staff*). Contains 1–2 internal Focus points that can be spent to cast a Working without marking personal Exert.

## 7. Contrast Summary: Tringad vs. Aurel

| Aspect | Tringad (Civic Dark Fantasy) | Aurel (Sword & Sorcery) |
|---|---|---|
| **Tone** | Rain, cold brass, oxblood ink, bone paper | Sunlit tall grass, mythic ruins, ancient kingdoms |
| **Drift Pacing** | **Hesitation Drift:** advances only when the cell delays or fails | **Ambient Drift:** advances automatically every round (the weather) |
| **Field Identities** | Licensed Orders (*Seeker, Breaker, Warden, Rival, Broker, Shade*) | Ancient Legacies (*Vanguard, Esoteric Arts, Infiltration Arts, Sacred Covenants*) |
| **Magic Casting** | Anchored Sanctioned Workings (costs Exert, keeps Drift quiet) | Unanchored Castings (costs 0 Exert, accelerates scene Drift) |
| **Civic Frame** | Sumner's Ward, tollgates, water-ferries, ledgers | Forgotten horizons, edgeless vows, open ruins |
| **The Goal** | Return of the Quiet Day | Solve the breach and extract before the region is lost |
