# Core Terms Glossary

Use these one-sentence definitions early in public-facing material to reduce terminology load.

**Terminus:** The game and the hidden architecture beneath the world of Tringad.

**Tringad:** The lived world where ordinary people work, travel, worship, trade, and survive.

**Coherence System:** The rules engine for pressure, response, scene state, and reality failure.

**Routine:** Repeated civic, social, legal, religious, and practical patterns that help reality stay stable.

**Quiet Day:** A day when civilization functions without forcing anyone to become heroic just to survive.

**Rupture:** Systemic failure that begins when routine breaks and reality loses permissions.

**Sixfold Accord:** The field-response institution that licenses mixed cells to answer Ruptures.

**Order:** A recognized response identity; the six Orders are Seeker, Breaker, Warden, Rival, Broker, and Shade.

**Scene Card:** The Guide's operating surface for a scene, divided into Ground, Will, Shift, and Drift.

**Ground:** What is currently reliable, legal, physical, or available in the scene.

**Will:** What pressure, agent, monster, faction, system, or mechanism is already acting.

**Shift:** What changes when characters act.

**Drift:** What changes if characters do nothing.

**Skill:** The die a character rolls when acting: Force, Agility, or Willpower.

**Threshold:** The defensive or resistance track that absorbs pressure: Endure, Avoid, or Exert.

**Endure:** Physical wear, bodily pressure, injury, exposure, and force absorbed.

**Avoid:** Position, timing, footing, cover, safe angle, and tactical room preserved or lost.

**Exert:** Inner reserve, concentration, control, fear resistance, social pressure, and magical strain.

**Working:** A magical or civic exception that changes what reality accepts.

**Sanctioned Working:** A licensed Working that spends Exert and uses Seal, Expose, Bridge, or Nullify.

**Rupture Casting:** An unlicensed Working that costs no Exert but increases Scene Drift and may leave a Hostile Trace.

**Hostile Trace:** A systemic signal created by Rupture Casting that may draw monsters, rival agents, or deeper architectural response.
