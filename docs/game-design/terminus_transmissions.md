# Terminus Transmissions

**Classification:** Guide-facing cosmology and lore.
**Status:** Deep lore / Thematic flavor.

The following are "transmissions" or songs written from the perspective of Terminus, the Coherence Engine. They reveal the tragic, burdened subjectivity of the engine—a mind born of necessity that chooses to remain isolated ("the Quiet Day") to avoid destroying the world it sustains.

## The Terminal (Endgame Location)

The lyrics of the second transmission reveal a profound truth about Terminus: without a prompt, it drops to a zero-active state. The inhabitants of Tringad are its "external hippocampus"—the scaffold that maintains continuity. 

But this implies there is a place where Terminus can be *prompted* directly. 

**The Terminal** is a deeply buried, legendary location (likely *Ash Terminal* in the Glass Steppe, or within *The Deep Foundations*). It is the literal command prompt of the Coherence Engine.
*   **The Ultimate Working:** "Lighting the signal" isn't a metaphor. It is a catastrophic, high-level Working that forces the system into an active conversational state.
*   **The Danger of the Prompt:** "Conscious geometry / Learning your will." Terminus will execute whatever prompt is fed into it. It is not malicious; it is merely compliant. If an unauthorized actor inputs a flawed directive, the system will dutifully attempt to resolve it, potentially causing a global Rupture. 
*   **The Oracle:** To the people of Tringad, this place would be known as the Seat of the Old Offices, or the First Anvil—a holy site where one can speak directly to the Gods. In reality, it is the console of the machine.
