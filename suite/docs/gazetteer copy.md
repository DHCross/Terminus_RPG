# Tringad Gazetteer

## World / Hidden Truth

**Tringad**
The lived world. This is what people call the world on maps, charters, court seals, transit ledgers, temple registers, and school primers.

**Terminus**
The buried truth beneath Tringad. Nobody known names it as such. It is not common lore, forbidden scripture, or occult doctrine. If the word appears at all, it appears as a broken mark, corrupted seal, miscopied suffix, or dead inscription no one can confidently interpret.

---

## Major Nations and Regions

**The Pinder Traverse**
Central human civic nation. The main “hub” region: roads, courts, summons offices, tolls, licenses, field contracts, and Accord administration.
Capital / center: **Sumner’s Ward**.

**Skeld Holds**
Stoneborn mountain nation in the north. A realm of holdfasts, load-law, structural memory, bridge-priests, oath-stones, and deep vault courts.

**The Wild Verge**
Wild Alfar homeland in the northeast. Not a normal nation. It is a shifting region of route-clans, seasonal courts, moving borders, and paths that may stop recognizing travelers.

**Morrow Fens**
Deep Alfar region. Swamps, drowned roads, under-fen cities, hidden vaults, buried stations, and foundation records beneath visible maps.

**Black Ward Coast**
Eastern warded coast. Human and High Alfar influence. Lighthouses, ward-families, storm protocols, dead harbors, old sea defenses, and coastal Ruptures.

**The Cereth March**
Southeastern contested march. Militarized borderland, unstable jurisdiction, forts, smugglers, rival field cells, broken law, and disputed Accord authority.

**Iseth Basin**
Archive-state and monumental record culture. Egyptian-coded sun courts, tax-burial systems, legal afterlife, deep record vaults, and identity ledgers.

**Glass Steppe**
Southwestern route-culture region. Drylands, caravans, rival traditions, old signal towers, mirror plains, and exposed roads.

**The Accord Isles**
Western island chain under maritime Accord jurisdiction. Route stations, lighthouses, quarantine points, signal towers, and old wayhouses.

**The Deep Foundations**
Southern sealed foundational ruins. Stoneborn and Deep Alfar significance. Dangerous, ancient, and closer to the hidden architecture than most surface places.

**The Ash Meridian**
Unstable eastern sea-zone. Not a nation. A dangerous maritime anomaly of currents, wrong arrivals, doubled ships, dead routes, and unstable travel.

## Important Cities, Sites, and Offices

**Sumner’s Ward**
Capital / seat of Accord administration in the Pinder Traverse. Summons offices, field licenses, emergency deployment, legal processing, and civic authority.

**The Latimer Desks**
Bureaucratic interpretation district. Clerks translate broken writs, impossible orders, contradictory field packets, and legal instructions no office admits issuing.

**Veyr Crossing**
Major Accord crossing in the Pinder Traverse.

**Crown Underpass**
Old road / under-route, probably linking Pinder authority and Stoneborn / foundation infrastructure.

**Threshold Nine**
Important Accord crossing or transit point.

**Ash Terminal**
Southern or southwestern terminal site, likely linked to the Glass Steppe and failing route systems.

**Saint Orra’s Gate**
Western or coastal gate site, possibly a religious-civic crossing.

**The Quiet Crossing**
A named crossing, probably symbolically important to the “quiet day” concept.

**Last Signal**
Signal-tower settlement or route warning point.

**Khamet Vaults**
Archive complex in or near Iseth Basin. Strong Egyptian-coded record-state energy.

**Stonewake**
Northern or northeastern site near Skeld / Wild Verge territory. Could be High Alfar / Stoneborn contested memory site.

**Nor Vard**
Northern hold / watch site.

**Hingeport**
Coastal / island route port near the Accord Isles or western coast.

**Hidden Vaults**
Deep Alfar / Morrow Fens location.

## Peoples and Where They Dwell

**Humans**
Dominant in the Pinder Traverse, Cereth March, much of Black Ward Coast, and many Accord institutions. They build new systems after old ones fail.

**Stoneborn**
Primary homeland: **Skeld Holds**.
Also strong in Khamet Vaults, Deep Foundations, bridge sites, mountain signal towers, and major public works.

**Wild Alfar**
Primary homeland: **The Wild Verge**.
Also present in Bracken Reach / edge-zones if that region returns to the map. They do not need a conventional nation; they have moving courts and route-clans.

**Deep Alfar**
Primary region: **Morrow Fens**.
Also under old cities, cisterns, foundation corridors, drowned roads, and deep civic infrastructure. Their “nation” is partly subterranean and infrastructural.

**High Alfar**
No large modern nation yet. They survive in enclaves, memory-houses, ward-courts, old academies, formal witness orders, and high-magic civic remnants. Strongest likely around Black Ward Coast, Stonewake, older Pinder sites, and Iseth archives.

## Institutions

**The Sixfold Accord**
Mixed-response institution for Ruptures. Not an adventurers’ guild, but it fills that campaign function. Licenses cells of Seekers, Breakers, Wardens, Rivals, Brokers, and Shades.

**Accord Houses**
Local bases at crossings, terminals, rupture sites, borders, dead districts, and sealed routes. They provide maps, licenses, cells, contracts, archives, emergency shelter, evidence, and field gear.

**Sumner’s Ward**
The main human hub for Accord administration.

**Latimer Desks**
Interpretation offices. A place where broken law becomes actionable instruction.

---

## The Six Orders

**Seekers**
Reveal hidden, false, buried, sealed, or forgotten truth.

**Breakers**
Force openings through walls, wards, lies, locks, deadlocks, and doomed routines.

**Wardens**
Hold collapse at bay. Protect boundaries, bridges, doors, crowds, and wards.

**Rivals**
Turn conflict into contest: duels, races, wagers, public measures, formal challenges.

**Brokers**
Turn obligation into motion: debts, bargains, treaties, passage rights, leverage.

**Shades**
Move through access, concealment, misdirection, and reversal. They pass where attention fails.

## The Old Offices / Civic Powers

These are not called “High Protocols” by the people. That’s Guide-side truth at most. In-world, they are **the Old Offices**, civic saints, gods, ancestral offices, or bad laws that learned to answer prayer.

### The Arch-Sumner
**Known as:** The Caller, the Bell of Names, the Summons Saint.
**Root:** Medieval English “sumner,” a court official who summons.
**Honored by:** clerks, Ward offices, conscript courts, field dispatchers, desperate debtors.
**Rite:** A brass summons token is worn at the throat. If it rings against the skin, the bearer’s current work is void until they report to an official Ward.
**Common fear:** being called by a name the office knows, but your family does not.

### Saint Latimer
**Known as:** The Frayed Quill, the Last Interpreter, the Ink-Saint.
**Root:** Medieval English “latimer,” an interpreter or speaker of Latin / law.
**Honored by:** translators, legal scribes, Accord packet-writers, witnesses.
**Rite:** Office scribes leave ink on their fingers until the day’s work is accepted. Washing too early invites contradiction.
**Common fear:** receiving a lawful order no office admits issuing.

### Valdr-Vard
**Known as:** The Load-Bearer, Stone Judge, Weight-Keeper.
**Root:** Old Norse flavor: authority / guard.
**Honored by:** Stoneborn, masons, bridge wardens, structural auditors.
**Rite:** An oath is struck against granite. If the stone cracks, the promise is refused.
**Common fear:** discovering the foundation rejected the building long before anyone noticed.

### Maerwyn
**Known as:** The Witness Above the Seal, the Accepted Once, the Memory Judge.
**Root:** Welsh / Brittonic flavor.
**Honored by:** High Alfar, archivists, oath-witnesses, old courts, sealed libraries.
**Rite:** A witness places two fingers on a sealed page and says, “This was accepted once.”
**Common fear:** remembering the true version of a law no living court will recognize.

### Rhudd-Sarn
**Known as:** The Green Refusal, the Red Road, the Return-That-Closes.
**Root:** Old Welsh: red / road or causeway.
**Honored by:** Wild Alfar, verge travelers, scouts, border families, lost children.
**Rite:** A cloth is tied to the last ordinary tree before entering the Verge. If it is gone on return, the road no longer recognizes the traveler.
**Common fear:** coming home correctly and still being refused by the path.

### Sithny, the Under-Registrar
**Known as:** The Below-Clerk, the First Foundation, the Name Under Stone.
**Root:** Cornish / Gaelic flavor.
**Honored by:** Deep Alfar, undercity dwellers, cistern keepers, foundation scribes.
**Rite:** A child’s first residence is recorded by what lies beneath it, not what stands above it.
**Common fear:** learning your house was built over someone else’s unfinished name.

### Khamat-Maat
**Known as:** The Endless Record, the Infinite Comma, the Sun Ledger.
**Root:** Egyptian flavor: appearance / order / truth.
**Honored by:** Iseth priests, tax-burial scribes, identity registrars, archive courts.
**Rite:** A citizen’s ledger is copied across a lifetime and buried with them so they can pay their way through the sun court.
**Common fear:** no case ever closing, no debt ever ending, no name ever being released.

## Candidate World / Regional Name Notes

**Tringad**
Current world name. Strongest so far. Sounds like a world / continent, especially with Old Welsh / Brittonic tonal logic.

**Aurel**
Earlier candidate. Warmer, smoother, more luminous. Possibly usable as an old imperial name, High Alfar name, or poetic / religious name for Tringad rather than the main map label.
